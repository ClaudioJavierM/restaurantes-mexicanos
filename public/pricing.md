# FAMER Restaurant Listing Pricing

## Free (Unclaimed)
- Price: $0/month
- Features: Basic listing, address, phone, category
- Limits: No analytics, no owner tools

## Claimed (Free)
- Price: $0/month  
- Features: Owner-verified profile, respond to reviews, update hours/photos
- Limits: No featured placement, no analytics dashboard

## Premium
- Price: $9.99 first month, then $29/month
- Annual: $290/year (save 17%)
- Features:
  - Featured placement in search results (4x more visibility)
  - Full analytics dashboard (views, clicks, profile visits)
  - Online reservation management
  - Priority support
  - Premium badge on listing
- Limits: No chatbot, no elite placement

## Elite
- Price: $79/month
- Annual: $790/year
- Features:
  - Top placement in all searches (above Premium)
  - Everything in Premium
  - AI Chatbot 24/7 (answers questions automatically)
  - Advanced analytics with competitor comparison
  - Dedicated account manager
